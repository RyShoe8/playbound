{{TOTAL}} large mods across {{GAMES}} published games, taken from ModDB and Nexus Mods. Tick Mod to add it to the game's mod list. Tick Edition on total conversions you want listed as an edition. {{EDITIONS}} are flagged as edition candidates.

Counts are visits or downloads on the source site, not PlayBound installs. Mods whose descriptions were thin on the source page have short summaries; full descriptions, file lists, screenshots and requirements are collected for the approved ones next.
